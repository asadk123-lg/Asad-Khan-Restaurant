The Philosophy of Khan's Kitchen

❖ ❖ ❖

Founded with an unwavering pledge: never compromise on the purity of the meat, the warmth of firewood embers, or the sacred tradition of hospitality.

Shinwari cuisine is renowned across the world because it relies on the purest quality of fresh meat. In our kitchen, meat is never frozen. We simmer our legendary Karahis only in natural animal fat, luscious ripe tomatoes, and Himalayan rock salt—allowing the genuine essence of lamb and beef to reign supreme.

Frequently Asked Questions

Authentic Shinwari Karahi is prepared using the pure Pashtun tradition: only fresh hand-cut lamb or mutton, tomatoes, green chillies, and rock salt, cooked in natural lamb fat or desi ghee. Unlike commercial curries, there is no onion gravy or artificial powdered masala, allowing the rich natural taste of the meat to shine through.

Because we only cook fresh meat on order in front of live firewood and wok flames, Shinwari Karahi takes approximately 30 to 45 minutes to tenderize to absolute melt-in-the-mouth perfection. You can also pre-order when reserving your table so it arrives hot when you sit!
